# Thirty ideas for Holdout

From the biggest to the smallest. None of these are built yet. Three ideas
from the first draft of this list (runs together, a camp that lives without
its host, and pings) turned out too good to leave for later and shipped in v4;
the three below that take their places are new.

## Huge

1. **A shared city.** Always-on camps on one server share one city. Meet
   another camp's squad in a building, trade at a neutral market, help each
   other through a horde or race them to a story lead.
2. **Server seasons.** Month-long events for every camp on a server at once: a
   winter where the same great horde comes for everyone on the same night,
   with a board of who held and what it cost them.
3. **Rival camps.** Other survivor groups on the city map, run by the game or
   by other players: they trade, steal from outposts, race you to story items
   and sometimes attack.
4. **Act two: the coast.** After the evacuation, the story continues at Port
   Halden: a new map, boats instead of vans, a quarantine zone with its own
   politics, and harder infected.
5. **Killzones and traps.** Build the ground the horde walks over: spike
   strips, pits, barbed wire, gates, funnels and mazes that the zombie
   pathfinding has to respect, tower-defence style.
6. **Survivor relationships.** Friendships, rivalries and romances that change
   morale and how well people work together, with drama events: arguments,
   theft, people leaving together, someone covering for a friend.
7. **A city that changes.** Fires that spread and gut blocks, buildings that
   collapse, hordes that migrate between districts, roads that flood in spring
   and close in winter.
8. **Driving.** Take the wheel on the city map: drive the van along the
   streets, stop for fuel, push through roadblocks, escort convoys to
   outposts.

## Big

9. **Power lines you lay.** Poles with a range, cables you route, overloads
   that blow a fuse and black out a section, like Satisfactory's grid.
10. **Fluids.** Pumps, pipes and tanks for water and diesel, a water tower, a
    refinery that cracks crude from the highway tankers.
11. **Farming depth.** Several crops (corn, potatoes, herbs that become
    medicine), compost and fertiliser, a greenhouse for winter, chickens and
    goats.
12. **Cooking.** Real recipes at the kitchen with buffs: stew for morale,
    coffee for work speed, preserved food that lasts the winter.
13. **Injuries with names.** Broken bones, deep cuts and concussions, each with
    its own treatment, recovery time and lasting scars.
14. **A dog.** A scout dog for runs that smells zombies through walls, finds
    hidden stashes and warns the camp at night.
15. **Radio choices.** Calls that ask for a decision: answer bandits, trade
    with strangers, take in a family that might be infected.
16. **Bosses.** Named infected at landmark locations (the hospital, the mall,
    the stadium) with unique loot and their own behaviour.

## Medium

17. **Weather on runs.** Thunder that masks noise, fog that hides you and
    them, rain that puts out molotovs, snow that leaves tracks.
18. **Weapon attachments.** Scopes, silencers, bigger magazines and grips as
    parts you craft or find and fit at the gunsmith.
19. **Blueprints.** Rare plans found in the city for unique station variants
    (a military field kitchen, an industrial furnace) with better rates.
20. **Generations.** Survivors age, retire from runs, teach the young, and
    children are born in camp: a camp that outlives its founders.
21. **Dawn report.** One summary card each morning: what was made, what ran
    out, which belts backed up, who got hurt.
22. **Camp history.** A timeline and graphs of the whole camp: population,
    kills, milestones and deaths day by day, with notable moments.
23. **Seasonal operations.** Month-long challenges with small unique rewards,
    such as a winter siege or a supply-drop race.

## Small

24. **Photo mode.** Free camera, filters, depth of field and a postcard export
    of your camp.
25. **Tapes and music.** A radio in camp that plays tapes found on runs, and
    music layers that follow the time of day and the danger.
26. **Assign best.** One button on a station to fill it with the best free
    survivors, and a hotkey that cycles through idle ones.
27. **A banner.** Name your camp and paint a flag for the gate and the map.
28. **Faces that remember.** Portraits that show bandages, scars and the gear
    someone wears.
29. **Achievements.** Small cosmetic unlocks: gate styles, banner colours,
    a gold belt.
30. **Quick calls.** A wheel of short calls for runs together ("Over here",
    "Need help", "Pulling back") with a crackle of radio.
