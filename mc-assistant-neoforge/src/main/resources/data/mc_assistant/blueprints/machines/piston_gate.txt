# [redstone] The piston gate: a two-wide, two-high door in the wall, of the wall's own stone, on four sticky
# pistons, worked by a lever on the inside of the wall beside it.
#
# Thrown, the lever powers the block it is fixed to. That block powers the upper left piston beside it and the
# redstone on top of it, which climbs over a slab (a slab carries no power, so the line is not cut) and runs
# along the top of the gate under a capping course to the far post; each block it runs over is powered, and
# with it the upper right piston under its end. Each lower piston comes with the one above it (a piston is also
# moved by power at the block over it, the game's own rule). The four push their four blocks into the doorway,
# and it is shut tight. Thrown back, the sticky pistons draw the blocks back into the wall, and the way is open:
# two wide and two high, for folk and players. The front of the drawing is the inside of the town, where the
# guards come to the lever. Laid in the wall's main gate, it takes the place of the wooden doors (they go back
# to the stores); the pillars either side of the gate keep their lanterns on top.
