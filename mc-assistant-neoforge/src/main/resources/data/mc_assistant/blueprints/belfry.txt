# stone twenty-two courses high, posts up its corners, slit windows up every face and a gilded clock front
# and back. A ladder climbs the back wall to the bell chamber, open on every side under arches with a rail

# a stone spire with a gilded ball, a cross and a lamp at the tip, a lamp on each corner's pinnacle, and a
