# [civic-land] A knight's hall: the grander manor a knight pays the town for (KnightManors), on the frame of the
# Iron Age's manor house. Two full storeys on a stone footing under a slate roof, glass all round. Below, the great
# room: the long carpet, the table and the stove, chests at the back, bookshelves along the walls, casks by the door.
# Above, the knight's chambers: two beds (the knight's and a guest's), shelves of books, casks, lanterns from the beams.
# [build-looks] The middle bay of its front is dressed stone from the footing up, and over the door rises the
# knight's tower above the ridge, a window to the street at each floor of it, a slate cap and merlons round its
# top: the knight's hall is known from the knight's manor (the same frame, smaller) by it.
