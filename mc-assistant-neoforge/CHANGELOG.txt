2026-07-b286 · THE CAVE TEAM. The cave dwellers are now a small, highly skilled team: two from twenty-five folk in the Iron Age, three at sixty, four at a hundred, picked from the town's best-levelled miners and guards (never an idle new hand, never from a trade the town is short of), the most experienced leading; as hardy as the watch, and among the best paid in the town for dangerous, skilled work underground. On a long trip they make camp at dusk in a nook walled in and lit, one on watch. A day overdue, a search party goes.
