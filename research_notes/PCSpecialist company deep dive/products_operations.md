# PCSpecialist (UK): Products, Pricing, Operations, Supply Chain and Support

Research note (compiled 3 November 2026). Method caveat: direct page fetches of pcspecialist.co.uk, pcspecialist.ie and overclock3d.net failed in this session because those hosts are outside the network allowlist, and the Wayback Machine could not be used. Everything below comes from web-search result snippets and summaries of the cited pages. Treat exact wording and prices as "observed via search index, November 2026". Where a page's age was visible in the index, it is noted. Prices are in GBP and include VAT unless stated otherwise.

## 1. Product ranges and named lines (desktops, laptops, workstations, business, mini PCs) and how the line-up evolved

### Takeaway
PCSpecialist is a build-to-order integrator. Its catalogue has two layers:
- **Configurator "ranges"**: desktop families such as Gaming PCs, Liquid Series (water-cooled), Lumin Series (all-white), Video Editing, Pro Audio and Professional Workstations, plus laptop series Recoil, Ionico, Defiance/Defiance Pro, Elimina/Elimina Pro and Lafité/Lafité AI.
- **Named "featured" pre-specified builds**: reviewer and retailer models such as Cortex, Nebula, Hyperion, Noble, Storm, Falcon, Zircon Nano, Element, Momentum, Frostbyte, Helios and Vortex, often with Pro, Elite, Master, Extreme or Ultimate suffixes.

The laptops are rebadged white-label chassis from both Tongfang and Clevo, and the chassis can change within a single series name.

## 2. How the configurator works; entry, mid and high-end price points; pre-built and fast-dispatch options

Customers choose a base chassis or series and configure CPU, GPU, RAM, storage, cooling, OS and warranty. Observed entry points (November 2026) are £480 for home/office desktops, £560 for gaming/pro desktops and £820–£1,930 for laptop series. Reviewed configurations cluster at about £1,000–£1,900 for mid-range and £2,700 and up for RTX 5080/5090 builds. ## 3. Manufacturing and fulfilment: location, volumes, QC/testing, build and dispatch times, delivery partners, international shipping

### Takeaway
Systems are built in-house in Wakefield, West Yorkshire. The company has existed since 2003 and builds more than 100,000 systems a year, per OC3D's December 2021 visit. Every system gets a bespoke stress-test suite, and liquid-cooled builds also get a 24-hour leak test and a 48-hour burn-in. DPD is the main UK courier. A fulfilment and customer-service centre opened in Heerlen, Netherlands in 2022 to serve Europe.

### Cited Findings
- "PC Specialist is based in Wakefield and has been building high-end systems since 2003" — [Trusted Reviews](https://www.trustedreviews.com/reviews/laptops/pc-specialist)
- OC3D visited "PCSpecialist's facility in Wakefield" (articles from December 2021). It reports that a PCSpecialist computer is delivered "every 4.8 minutes", which it equates to over 100,000 computers a year. An earlier piece says "over 100,000 PCs are built here every year" — [OC3D: We visited PCSpecialist and took a peek inside](https://overclock3d.net/reviews/misc_content/we-visited-pcspecialist-and-took-a-peek-inside/); [OC3D: We Visited PCSpecialist – Here's what we found](https://overclock3d.net/reviews/systems/we-visited-pcspecialist-heres-what-we-found/)
- QC process, as reported by OC3D (December 2021):
  - Every PC and laptop gets a bespoke suite of tests: heavy CPU and GPU loads, multiple reboot loops and other fault-finding tests. Any fault found is fixed and the system is retested.
  - Liquid-cooled systems get a 24-hour leak and pressure test before power-on. They are then tweaked, overclocked and stability-tested.
  - After Windows and drivers are installed, liquid builds undergo a 48-hour burn-in.

  Source: [OC3D visit, page 2](https://overclock3d.net/reviews/systems/we_visited_pcspecialist_-_here_s_what_we_found/2)
- Official FAQ (indexed copy about 8 years old):
  - Building usually takes 1–2 working days, and testing up to 2 working days (4 for liquid systems). Weekends and bank holidays are excluded.
  - DPD is the main courier. Standard 24-hour Monday–Friday delivery is free to most UK postcodes, with surcharges for the Scottish Highlands and islands.

  Source: [PCSpecialist FAQs](https://www.pcspecialist.co.uk/faqs/)
- Third-party lead-time estimates (vary; not official):
  - 5–7 working days for desktops and 3–5 for laptops (Picodi, about 2 years old) — [Picodi](https://www.picodi.com/uk/pc-specialist)
  - Build 3–5 working days, plus 2–3 working days' delivery to Ireland (July 2026 guide) — [CustomPCParts.ie](https://custompcparts.ie/guides/pcspecialist-ireland-review/)
  - One buyer received a PC within 5 working days (October 2024) — [The Outer Haven](https://www.theouterhaven.net/2024/10/my-first-gaming-pc-from-pcspecialist-review/)
  - Customer reviews range from "4 day build as promised and then next day delivery" to "eighteen days from order to delivery" — [reviews.io](https://www.reviews.io/company-reviews/store/pcspecialist/KOW)
- European operations:
  - "In 2022 we opened a fulfilment centre in Heerlen, The Netherlands which also handles customer service and support." The NL office is at Trompenburgstraat 4, 6412 ZH Heerlen.
  - The company has a subsidiary in Germany and registered branches in France and Italy. A later version adds Spain and says it is "registered for VAT in 13 countries".

  Sources: [PCSpecialist About Us](https://www.pcspecialist.co.uk/about-us/); [PCSpecialist Contact Us](https://www.pcspecialist.co.uk/contact-us/); [Trustpilot company profile](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/pcspecialist.co.uk)

### Inferences
- Builds appear to be centralised in Wakefield. The Heerlen site is described as a fulfilment and customer-service centre, not a build site, so EU orders are likely built in the UK and distributed or supported via the Netherlands. The Heerlen opening in 2022 was plausibly a post-Brexit customs and VAT response. That rationale is not stated in the sources.
- Typical real-world lead time for a custom desktop seems to be about 3–7 working days plus 1–2 days' delivery. Peaks and component constraints stretch this to 2–3 weeks.

### Gaps
- No current (2024–2026) official build-volume, headcount or factory-size figures were found. The 100k-per-year figure dates from December 2021.
- European courier partners (outside the UK and DPD) were not confirmed.
- Non-EU shipping (e.g. Switzerland, Norway) was not confirmed, although Galaxus sells in Austria and Switzerland.

## 4. Supply chain and vendor partnerships; effect of the 2020–22 GPU shortage and 2025–26 DRAM/NAND price spikes

## 5. Warranty and aftercare: tiers, returns, support channels, repair turnaround

### Takeaway
PCSpecialist UK offers four collect-and-return warranty tiers, all with 3 years' labour and lifetime hardware technical support:

| Tier | Parts | Collection |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 1 year | 6 months |
| Silver | 1 year | 1 year |
| Gold | 2 years | 2 years |
| Platinum | 3 years | 3 years |

Outside the collection period, service is return-to-base at the customer's cost. Consumers have a 14-day cancellation right from delivery. Support runs by email, phone, live chat and the company forum. Anecdotal repair turnaround ranges from about a week to a month.
