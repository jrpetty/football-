*Method note (Nov 2026): this session's network policy blocked direct page fetches of Companies House (find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk), pcspecialist.co.uk / pcspecialist.ie and web.archive.org. Every finding below therefore comes from web-search result extracts of those pages and of secondary aggregators (Endole, Pomanda, D&B, openregister.de, etc.), not from a full read of the primary page. The Companies House-derived facts (dates, numbers, PSC change) were consistent across several extracts, but the report writer should treat exact dates as "per search extract of Companies House". ## 1. When, where and by whom was PCSpecialist founded, and what were its early years like?

### Takeaway
PC Specialist Limited (company no. 04777867) was incorporated on 27 May 2003 in West Yorkshire, England. Its founder and only director ever recorded is Daniel Martin "Danny" Williams. His Companies House-listed birth month and year (January 1985) mean he was about 18 when he started the business. Its first known premises were small units at Brockholes Business Park near Holmfirth, about 5 miles south of Huddersfield. No first-hand origin-story interview was found.
